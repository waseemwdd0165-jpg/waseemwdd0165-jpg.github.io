How it works

  • The map is dark. You see only what your torch lights, plus a pace or two around your feet.
  • Your torch points where you are moving, so you cannot look one way and walk another.
  • One player starts as the hunter. Everyone else is a runner.
  • Runners win by reaching the green exit. A faint marker at the screen edge points the way.
  • A caught runner joins the hunt, so the odds shift every time somebody falls.
  • Three rounds. Escaping scores 100, catching scores 50.
  • The host has to keep this page open. Closing it or reloading ends the room, and the code stops working for everybody.
