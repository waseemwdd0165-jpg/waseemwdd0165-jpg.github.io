        Why this is harder than it looks

        Machine text is not worse text. It is text with a particular shape: balanced on both sides of a question, tidy where real life is messy, and closing with a sentence that restates the opening. Human writing commits to things, names specifics, and stops when the point is made. Once you see that difference you cannot unsee it.
